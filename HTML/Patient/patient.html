      Post-Procedure Care
      After Root Canal

      Daily Habits
      The 2-Minute Rule

      Brush for at least two minutes, twice a day, to effectively remove plaque.

      Floss Daily

      Flossing removes plaque between teeth where a toothbrush cannot reach.

      Tongue Cleaning

      Clean your tongue daily to remove bacteria and maintain fresh breath.

      Diet & Nutrition
      The Water Rinse

      Rinse with water after meals to neutralize acids if you can't brush immediately.
